- [Poster PDF](AGU26_Token_GeoZhang.pdf) and [PNG preview](AGU26_Token_GeoZhang.png): export and quick review.

The poster describes **attempted** software-engineering work delegated to agents. Its energy and emissions estimates are conditional on the stated session demand, open-model GPU benchmark, facility overhead, and U.S. electricity mix. See the [source audit](numerical/SOURCE_AUDIT.md) before interpreting the numbers as measured labor replacement or commercial-provider emissions.
